Gomoku

Play the search bot, the learned bot or the self-play bot, or set up a position and ask for the best next move.

Five or more in a row wins. Columns A–O, rows 1–15 from the top. A red dot marks the last stone.

Opponent

Base is the search. Learned trained against that search on a 9×9 board, then against itself, and plays here on 15×15. Self-play trained on its own tree search and searches 200 playouts per move. All three take a win and block a loss immediately.

You play

Choosing a color starts a new game. Black opens.

Placing

Side to move
